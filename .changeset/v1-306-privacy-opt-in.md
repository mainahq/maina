All telemetry is opt-in, crash reports included (FR-PRIV-1), and `maina privacy` prints what may be sent (FR-PRIV-3). Core resolves one effective collection config (`loadCollectionConfig`, `isChannelEnabled`) for three channels, `crash_reports`, `usage` and `outcome_sharing`, through the injected fs and env ports: everything is off unless the user policy (`~/.maina/policy.json` `telemetry`) or a legacy `~/.maina/config.yml` flag (`errors: true`, `telemetry: true`) opts in; a repo policy can only opt out; `DO_NOT_TRACK=1`, `MAINA_TELEMETRY=0` or `~/.maina/telemetry.json` `{ "optOut": true }` switch everything off; an unreadable or invalid policy counts as off. Every outbound send goes through a new `NetworkPort`. Breaking: `isCliTelemetryOptedOut` is removed; `sendCliErrorReport` needs `{ fs, network }` and sends nothing without a `crash_reports` opt-in; `isErrorReportingEnabled(ctx)`, `isTelemetryEnabled(ctx)`, `reportError` and `trackUsageEvent(ctx, ...)` are async and read through the ports instead of `homedir()`/`node:fs`; `captureUsage`/`captureError` take a `TelemetryContext` (`{ fs, env, root? }`) and `createPosthogClient` reads consent through `fs`; setup telemetry is opted out unless the user opted in to `usage`, and `MAINA_TELEMETRY=1` or a repo `.maina/config.json` `telemetry: true` no longer opt in. New `shareOutcomes`/`buildOutcomeSharePayload` (FR-DEC-7) share a (decision, outcome) pair only with an `outcome_sharing` opt-in, as a closed payload of decision metadata (type, model hash, fixed-option answer, confidence, final action, latency, host) and the outcome label; `validateOutcomeSharePayload` rejects anything else.
