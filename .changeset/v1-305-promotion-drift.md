Add shadow mode, the promotion report and the drift guard for `decide` backends (FR-DEC-2, FR-DEC-8). `shadowRun` asks a candidate backend the same questions as the policy's backend, logs both (shadow records carry `finalAction: "shadow"`) and returns only the primary's decisions, so a shadow that disagrees, fails, throws or mutates its input never changes an action. `evaluatePromotion(slice, gates)` pairs shadow records with their primaries and reports every promotion metric the log can supply (samples, labelled, agreement, override rate, and for incumbent and candidate: error rate, expected cost from `error_costs`, calibration error, mean confidence, p50/p95 latency, decided-without-asking share, false-allow rate and reproducibility over repeated inputs), checks each gate (the usefulness, false-allow and reproducibility gates only when set) and promotes only when all pass; missing evidence fails its gate. The §9.2 metrics the log cannot supply (Brier/accuracy on the frozen set, ECE per length bucket, false-allow on destructive actions, order stability, injection resistance) are listed in `notFromLog`. Shadow records follow the log's privacy setting. `checkDrift(slice, thresholds)` watches a promoted backend's latest `drift.window` decisions and, when the error rate or confidence drop exceeds the policy limits, demotes the type to the backend it was promoted from (`previous`, else its catalog default) with a user notice; `applyDriftAction` applies the demotion to a policy. `readLogSlice` reads decisions and their outcomes for both.
